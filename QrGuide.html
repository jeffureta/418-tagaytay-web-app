Smart Lock & Keyless Check-In

Check-In: 2:00 PM • Check-Out: 12:00 NN

  1. Activate Keypad: Touch the black touchscreen with the palm of your hand or fingers until the digits light up.
  2. Enter PIN: Key in your assigned 6-digit access code sent via SMS/Email followed by the # key.
  3. Unlocking: The motor will beep and chime twice. Turn the handle downward to open.

Induction Cooker & Kitchenette

Single-burner induction stove operation

  • Place Compatible Pan First: Only use the provided stainless induction pots and pans stored in the lower cabinet. The burner will beep and display E0 if turned on without a pot.
  • Power On: Press the ON/OFF button, then select HEAT or FRY.
  • Adjust Heat: Use the + and - touch keys (levels 200W – 1800W).
  • Range Hood: Always switch on the overhead exhaust hood when cooking to keep Tagaytay mountain air crisp.

Shower Water Heater

Instant multi-point water heater

Stay warm in cool Tagaytay weather with instant hot showers:

  1. Ensure the dedicated wall switch (labeled WATER HEATER outside the bathroom door) is illuminated ON.
  2. Turn on the shower faucet to initiate water flow.
  3. Adjust the temperature dial on the heater unit: Medium (10 o'clock position) is comfortable for most guests.
